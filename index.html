How due dates work

Each card's due day repeats every month. Your twice-monthly plan schedules the final payment 2–3 days before the due date shown here.

Currency

Track money from multiple countries: every account, card, goal and investment has its own currency, chosen when you add or edit it. Totals are always shown separately per currency — amounts are never converted or added together.

Appearance

System follows your device's light / dark setting. The 🌙 / ☀️ button in the header switches instantly; choosing it here or there saves the same preference on this device only.

Payments

Each card is split into two payments a month. The final payment is scheduled this many days before the due date you enter per card.

Local reminders

Checks run whenever you open the app: cards due within 3 days, and savings goals off pace.

Export

UTF-8 with BOM, CRLF line endings — opens correctly in Excel, Numbers, Google Sheets and LibreOffice on any OS.

Backup & restore

Your data lives only in this browser (local-first). Keep a backup file somewhere safe.

Cloud backup (Google Drive)

Encrypted backup in your own Google Drive, inside a “Finance App” folder. The backup is encrypted on this device with your passphrase before it is uploaded — Google cannot read it, and the passphrase is never stored. See GOOGLE-DRIVE-SETUP.md for the one-time setup.

Lose this passphrase and the cloud backup cannot be read — there is nothing to reset. Keep it somewhere safe, separate from the backup.
